# HAVK: Hacking the Desert – Hackathon Overview

## General Information
- **Name**: HAVK: Hacking the Desert
- **Organizer**: HAVK (CoFounders: Summer Malik & Vedanth Shenoy)
- **Audience**: Open to developers, engineers, and tech enthusiasts of all levels
- **Duration**: 1 week
- **Format**: Online, software-focused (no hardware required)
- **Theme**: Address any form of scarcity (water, food, energy, healthcare access, digital equity, resource distribution, etc.) through software innovation.
- **Registration**:
  - Join the HAVK Discord: <https://discord.gg/EBXR4SuBCT>
  - Fill out the registration form: <https://forms.gle/bobG8g2tgNza7aU98>

---

## Challenge / Problem Statement
Use code to develop impactful solutions to real-world scarcity issues. Interpret “scarcity” broadly and build something meaningful.

### Suggested Focus Areas (non-exhaustive)
1. Tech solutions for food deserts or water scarcity
2. Apps for supply-chain resilience in remote or unstable regions
3. Systems that uplift marginalized and underserved communities
4. Smart resource management for urban or rural "resource deserts"
5. Bio-hacks for drought-resistant agriculture
6. Emergency technologies for climate-driven migration zones

> These are only suggestions—original, unconventional ideas are highly encouraged.

---

## Required Deliverables
1. **5-Minute Video Presentation**
   - Explain product, idea, and process
   - Keep it under 5 minutes (clear & concise)
   - Upload to YouTube (unlisted OK) or share a file-hosting link
2. **GitHub Repository (Mandatory)**
   - All code must start from the hackathon start date
   - Use clear, consistent commit messages
   - Include a comprehensive **README** (setup, tech stack, idea summary)
3. **Project Description Document**
   - What the project does
   - Who it helps
   - How it works (brief technical overview)
   - Why it matters
